The body as a vessel
for stories.

A central element of my visual language is the human body as a vessel for stories, inviting viewers to engage with the complexities of identity, memory, and transformation. Each etching becomes a unique reflection on both personal and universal experiences, creating a space for introspection and connection.

Since 2023, I have been working with etching. Over time, my appreciation for analogue and traditional printmaking techniques has continued to grow, inspiring me to deepen my understanding of historical processes and to expand my technical practice through ongoing experimentation and learning.

Collective House B

Illustration

Collective House B is a shared creative space in Hildesheim. These illustrations were created in dialogue with the community that inhabits it — where image-making becomes a collective act, and individual voice finds resonance within a shared context.
