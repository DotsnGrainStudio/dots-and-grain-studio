The New Love Experience × LAIZ
Photography · Documentary

Six years alongside
a band becoming.

Since 2019, I have worked alongside The New Love Experience, documenting and shaping their visual journey as they evolved from emerging artists into an internationally recognized band.

Their debut single Ela Partiu (Agogo Records), featured in The Guardian, marked a significant milestone in their growth and international recognition.
