The way I work is as important as the work itself.

These principles guide every collaboration, every conversation, and every creative decision.

Studio Principles ↘
01

Concept before
aesthetics.

Every project begins with a clear idea, thoughtful research, and a strategic framework. Visual form should emerge from meaning—not the other way around.

02

Connection before
representation.

Trust, dialogue, and collaboration always come before image-making. I portray only what I can genuinely relate to or have built a meaningful relationship with.

03

Some stories
are not ours
to tell.

I respect authorship, lived experience, and the boundaries of representation. Not every perspective should be interpreted by me, and not every story is mine to shape.

04

I do not
aestheticize suffering.

Pain, trauma, and vulnerability are never treated as visual spectacle. Every story deserves care, nuance, and respect.

05

I understand design
as a shared resource.

I use my skills, platform, and network to support, collaborate with, and create opportunities for others whenever possible.

06

Empathy is part
of the process.

Sensitivity, reflection, and active listening guide every stage of my creative practice—from research to execution.

07

Allyship is an
ongoing practice.

I strive to create work that respects people, communities, cultures, and the environments in which it exists. Learning, listening, and accountability remain essential parts of my practice.

08

I encounter
the world
with optimism.

I believe design has the power to challenge assumptions, foster connection, and create space for more thoughtful futures. At the same time, I recognize that meaningful change requires me to set boundaries, make conscious decisions, and act with integrity.
